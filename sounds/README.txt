Sound files of your own go in this folder. The game plays them if they are here and carries on without them if they are not.

    blackflash.mp3       the voice line when a Black Flash is the killing blow
    mahito-domain.mp3    looped while Self-Embodiment of Perfection is open (without it the game plays a drone it makes itself)

Cursed Judge (each of these replaces a sound the game otherwise makes itself):

    gavel.mp3            an ordinary gavel strike
    gavel-heavy.mp3      a strike with the gavel grown large
    gavel-slam.mp3       Justice Served coming down
    court.mp3            Deadly Sentencing convening
    sword.mp3            the Executioner's Sword being handed over, and drawn
    execute.mp3          the sentence carried out

The block (hold F), likewise:

    block.mp3            a blow stopped on your guard
    guard-break.mp3      your guard being broken

The Vengeful Artist's four strikes, one sound each (cut from a clip you supplied):

    vengeful-hit1.mp3    the first thrust
    vengeful-hit2.mp3    the second
    vengeful-hit3.mp3    the cut
    vengeful-hit4.mp3    the fourth (and Broil's slam, and the last of Catastrophe's twenty)
    vengeful-ult.mp3     the whole of the Final Chapter, nine seconds (cut from a second clip; its scene is timed to it)

    The clips they were cut from are of another game, so these stay in this copy: they are not committed (.gitignore) and build.py
    leaves them out of the one-file build. On the public site the game makes spear sounds of its own, and a Final Chapter of its own.

The names have to match exactly, and these have to be .mp3. For the domain music, .m4a, .ogg or .wav work as well (mahito-domain.m4a and so on).

To use the track from the anime's soundtrack ("Self-Embodiment of Perfection"), save your own copy of it here as mahito-domain.mp3.
The game does not ship with it, or with any sound from the anime, because they are copyrighted.
